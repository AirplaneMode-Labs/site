Demos

Every Airplane Mode Labs repository with a browser surface ships a public demo on GitHub Pages from Sprint 1 onward. Each demo shows only what works today and has a box titled "What this demo does not do yet". Everything runs in the browser tab: nothing is uploaded, there are no third-party scripts, fonts or trackers, and the only network requests are for the page's own files.

These statuses were written on 9 Nov 2026 from local builds served under /<repo>/ on a developer machine. The Pages URLs become reachable only after the owner enables Pages and pushes; nothing on this page was measured against the live URLs.

What a demo is allowed to claim

A demo page states its browser requirements with live feature detection, names the sprint in which each missing piece lands, and works under its sub-path with relative URLs only. Storage demos write only under their own prefix, because all six share the airplanemode-labs.github.io origin.

What this demo does not do yet

  • No search.
  • No RSS feed.
  • Benchmarks are unmeasured; every cell on the benchmarks page reads "not measured".
  • The demo statuses above are hand-written and dated, not measured at the live URLs; they are updated by hand after each deploy.
