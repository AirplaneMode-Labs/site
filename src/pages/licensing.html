Why

Changing the licence of an established project causes forks and backlash: HashiCorp moved to BSL and OpenTofu forked within weeks; Redis changed licence and Valkey forked under the Linux Foundation. The lesson in our plan is to pick the licence on day one and never relicense the core. Permissive Apache-2.0 also carries a patent grant that enterprises look for, and it keeps embedding simple for developers who ship JavaScript and WebAssembly to their users.

What is paid

The paid layer is the control plane, which runs on the customer's premises. Per the plan it covers:

  • a signed private model and data registry with an offline sync and import channel;
  • policy: model allow-lists per device class, prompt-injection rulesets and data-retention controls;
  • audit logs and a DPDP/GDPR-ready compliance export;
  • SSO and SCIM for the admin console;
  • fleet capability and health telemetry, aggregated locally and exported on the customer's terms;
  • certified, reproducible builds with an SBOM;
  • Indic model packs;
  • support SLAs.
